Game Design Document

1\. Overview



● Game Title: Rush to Class



● Genre: 3D Time Management – Exploration Game



● Target Audience: University students / casual players



● Summary:



The game is a 3D orientation-style time management game where the player must navigate through a university building and attend classes on time. Players must find the correct classrooms based on their schedule while racing against time. If the player enters the wrong classroom or arrives late, they fail the level. The game simulates the stressful and confusing experience of the first day at university.



● Key Features:



Time-based gameplay where players must reach classes before the timer ends



Exploration of a university floor with corridors and classrooms



Schedule system that shows class time and room numbers



Increasing difficulty with tighter time limits and more complex layouts



2\. Game Mechanics



● Core Gameplay:



The player controls a student during their first day at university. The objective is to attend each class on time by navigating through the building and finding the correct classroom.



The player receives a schedule showing the course name, classroom number, and class time. They must explore the floor, read room numbers, and reach the correct classroom before the timer runs out.



Entering the correct classroom progresses the game to the next class. Entering the wrong classroom or arriving late results in failure.



The gameplay focuses on navigation, observation, and time management.



● Controls:



W-A-S-D / Arrow Keys – Move character



Mouse – Look around



E – Interact / Enter classroom



Tab – Open schedule



Shift – Sprint



Esc – Pause menu



● Characters:



Player – The Student



Can move around the school corridors



Can sprint for a short time to reach classes faster



Can check their schedule to see the next class location



NPCs



Other Students



Walk through corridors



Can block paths or create crowd situations



● Objects:



Classroom Doors



Each door has a classroom number



Entering the correct classroom completes the objective



Schedule Panel



Displays class time, course name, and room number



Signs and Posters



Help players navigate the building



Corridor Obstacles



Groups of students or closed paths that slow movement



● Progression System:



The game progresses through multiple class sessions. Each new stage introduces harder navigation challenges.



Later stages may include:



Shorter time limits



More classrooms and corridors



Busier hallways with more NPC students



Players improve by learning the layout of the building and managing their time more efficiently.



● Difficulty Level:



Difficulty increases gradually as the game progresses. Early levels introduce basic navigation and the schedule system.



Later levels become more challenging with:



More complex hallway layouts



Less time to reach classes



Increased corridor traffic from NPC students



This progression encourages players to move faster and make quicker navigation decisions.



3\. Story and World



● Story Summary:



The game follows a new university student on their first day of classes. Feeling nervous and unfamiliar with the building, the student must quickly find the correct classrooms before the lectures start.



As the day progresses, the player experiences the pressure of managing time and navigating an unfamiliar environment.



● Characters:



The Student (Main Character)



A first-year university student attending their first day of classes. They are unfamiliar with the building layout and must rely on observation and quick thinking to reach the correct classrooms.



Other Students



Other students moving through the hallways represent the busy environment of the university. They create a realistic campus atmosphere.



● World:



The game takes place inside a university building floor that includes corridors, classrooms, and signage. The environment is designed to resemble a real academic building where students move between classes.



● Atmosphere:



The intended atmosphere is slightly stressful but engaging. Players should feel the urgency of reaching class on time while exploring the school environment.



4\. Visuals and Audio



● Art Style:



The game uses a simple semi-realistic 3D art style based on a university interior. The environment includes classrooms, corridors, doors, and signs to create a believable campus setting.



● Interface (UI):



The user interface displays essential elements such as:



Class schedule



Current objective



Countdown timer



The UI is designed to be simple and easy to read during gameplay.
